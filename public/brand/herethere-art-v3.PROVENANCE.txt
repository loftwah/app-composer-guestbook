Original App Composer cut-paper artwork — Here / there
Reviewed 17 October 2026.

Source: one generated raster, "tile-herethere-v3", produced through the fal.ai queue endpoint
openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image under MP's authorisation, request
01a147da-cb4a-7942-b530-d856e77f72ea. The request itself, its prompt, the cost basis and the
review of every rejected image are recorded in the owning App Composer repository at
public/brand/apps/provenance.json and .artwork/manifests/tiles-direction-a.json.
FAL_AI_API_KEY is read from the environment by scripts/generate-app-art.mjs; it is never printed,
never written to a manifest, never committed and never shipped to the browser.

Art direction: App Composer cut-paper screenprint — warm ivory paper #f4f0e8, graphite ink
#252923, burnt orange #d95022, and one muted permission-green stamp square as the only secondary.
Flat shapes, ink-lined paper edges, coarse screenprint grain, deliberate registration offset.
No shading, perspective, gloss, gradients or glow. Subject: one burnt-orange postbox with a single
ivory postcard entering the slot. The postcard is a promise of a contribution, never a claim that
photos exist.

Files in this repository, all exported from the master at review time:
- herethere-art-v3-master.png — the reviewed master, square.
- herethere-art-v3-480.webp and herethere-art-v3-960.webp — 480px and 960px page hero derivatives.
- herethere-share-v3.jpg — 1200x630 JPEG social derivative.
- herethere-share-v3.webp and herethere-share-v3-600.webp — 1200x630 and 600x315 WebP variants.
- apps/herethere-small-v3.webp — 96px navigation crop.

Dimensions are reserved in the markup. The illustration is explicitly separate from submitted
contributions; it is never presented as a real photo or a real guest.

Superseded in this release and removed from the repository: the 14 October 2026
guestbook-character-v2 gouache illustration (600/1200px WebP and its social crop), the
courtyard-ai-v1 social pair and the share-ai-v1 poster pair from the earlier generated set. They
mixed glossy postcard renders with the current cut-paper grammar. Their bytes, prompts and the
magpie-on-letterbox original remain in this repository's git history; the reviewed App Composer
record keeps the comparison note.
